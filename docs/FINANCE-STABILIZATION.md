# Finance stabilization

The normal Finance store loads only the signed-in user's rows, even when that user is an admin. PostgreSQL RLS still protects writes. Card bills use a cycle close date; they reconcile after card purchases change and on load across the complete recorded history. Paid invoices retain their actual amount. `pay_card_bill` books the cash expense and marks the invoice paid in one database transaction. A retry returns the same payment.

Loan and consortium counts now represent confirmed payments. The plan detail page records an installment as a linked cash expense through an atomic, retry-safe RPC. The form count can establish a historical baseline. Manual recurring bills create their next monthly occurrence when paid.

The dashboard distinguishes **cash outflow** from **consumption** by category. The month-end result projects the month's cash flow. The separate 30/60/90-day forecast starts with available account balances and includes pending bills, overdue bills on the next day, the open card invoice at its actual next due date, monthly manual bill recurrences, remaining loan and consortium installments, and income explicitly marked recurring. It is an estimate: it cannot anticipate unrecorded expenses, salary changes, or future card purchases.

Accounts have an effective opening-balance date and archive date. Budget limit changes keep their prior intervals; the Budgets screen can inspect the last 12 months. A linked account can fund only one goal to avoid counting its whole balance twice. The health score reports insufficient history when income is missing or fewer than two income months exist and shows its factor scores otherwise.

Transactions can import CSV (`Data;Valor;Descrição;Tipo` or English headers) and OFX/QFX. The preview excludes exact matches already in that account, and OFX `FITID` remains unique per account in the database. An expense category can be assigned to the whole batch. Check the preview and account before saving; import does not guess categories from merchant names.

The test persona is generated by `supabase/seed/qa-persona.sql` for an existing Auth user. Its password is intentionally absent from source control. Apply migrations before using either seed script.
