# Routine

Routine starts with habits. Create a habit, choose its weekdays, and mark it complete on the scheduled day or a past date. Upcoming dates are visible for planning and cannot be completed early. The central panel shows the selected day's scheduled habits. A small blue dot in the mini calendar means all scheduled habits were completed; a red dot means a past day was incomplete, or some habits were completed today. The date cells retain a neutral background.

Routine has its own navigation icon immediately after Finances and lives at `/routine`. The old `/calendar` address redirects to it. On desktop, the mini calendar sits at the bottom of the right day pane, after today's habits, savings goals, and bills. Picking a date opens that day in Routine; the date is reflected in the URL so it survives a refresh. On smaller screens the same calendar appears above the day's checklist inside Routine. The Home page and desktop day pane continue to show today's habits with the same completion state.

Weekdays use Monday=0 through Sunday=6. A new habit starts today, so it never creates artificial failures in the past. Changing its weekday schedule creates an effective-dated version beginning today; older dates retain their previous schedule. Renaming changes its label, and archiving removes it from future dates while preserving earlier history. Historic whole-day `calendar_days` marks and notes stay visible. On dates with no scheduled habits, those marks can still be corrected or cleared.

The tables `habits`, `habit_schedule_changes`, and `habit_completions` use per-user RLS, explicit Data API grants, and composite owner foreign keys. The UI derives completion totals from stored rows. See `supabase/migrations/20260927130955_routine_habits.sql`. The test, demo, and QA persona seeds include four months of realistic completions. The hosted QA login has been populated without replacing its existing Finance data.
